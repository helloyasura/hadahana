## Manuscript / rural substrate

- **තල්පත් කොළ** kendara as heirloom (same material culture as Buddhist manuscripts).  
- **Lith Illakkam** numerals in Kandyan ola astrology manuscripts (Colombo Museum catalogues; Kandy Museum notes).  
- Sinhala archaic numerals / hora clocks of the last Kandyan king — birth time once expressed in local hora systems.  
- Katapayadi and related numeration appear in medieval SL technical culture (context for how numbers were written, not a modern calc method).

## Mathematical backbone (shared with Jyotisha)

- Sidereal zodiac + Lahiri ayanamsa  
- 27 nakshatra × 4 pada  
- Vimshottari 120y dasha  
- Swiss Ephemeris / Drik ganita as modern replacement for lith arithmetic  

## Reliability tiers for Stage 2

1. **Primary:** Sinhala handbooks + consistent multi-source SL practitioner agreement (Lahiri, pada naming, dasa porondam list).  
2. **Secondary:** Modern SL apps’ documentation (GoodVibes, sinhalahoroscope) — useful, but commercial.  
3. **Cousin:** Pan-Indian nakshatra tables — cross-check syllables; prefer Laksara where they diverge.  
4. **Avoid as authority:** Western tropical horoscope columns; unsourced Facebook letter lists.
