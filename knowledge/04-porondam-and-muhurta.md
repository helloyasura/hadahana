# Porondam & muhurta — what a full Handahana life cycle uses

Naming is only the first ritual use of the kendraya. A complete knowledge map must include matching and timing.

## Dasa Porondam (දස පොරොන්දම්) — core 10

Based on the two persons’ **Moon / Janma Nekatha** geometry (Handahana.itgo, Lakpura, GoodVibes):

| # | Porondama | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nekath | Mind / life-force alignment |
| 2 | Gana | Deva / Manushya / Rakshasa temperament |
| 3 | Mahendra | Children / attachment / progeny wellbeing |
| 4 | Stree Deergha | Husband’s capacity to sustain wife’s welfare |
| 5 | Yoni | Physical / intimacy archetype (animal pairs; enmity lists matter) |
| 6 | Rashi | Chandra-rashi compatibility |
| 7 | Rashi Adhipathi | Friendship of Moon-sign lords |
| 8 | Vashya | Long-term attraction |
| 9 | Rajju | Physical/longevity “rope” — traditionally critical |
| 10 | Vedha | Afflictive “breaking” pairs of stars |

Common rule of thumb: **≥ 5/10** often treated as workable, but **Yoni** and **Rajju** failures are weighed heavily even if the total looks fine. Full charts + doshas (e.g. Kuja) still override a raw score.

## Visi Porondam (විසි පොරොන්දම්) — 20

Adds further nekatha groupings (Lakpura summary): Vruksha, Ayu, Pakshi, Bhuta, Gothra, Varna, Linga, Nadi, plus Dina (weekday from sunrise in Sinhala system) and Graha (whole-chart judgement).

Tamil **Pathu Porutham** is cousin but not identical (e.g. Dina counted on stars; some Rajju allowances differ).

## Muhurta / Subha Nekatha

Used for: Nam Thebima, indul kata gema, schooling start, travel, business opening, housewarming, wedding.

Panchanga limbs (පංචාංග):

1. **Tithi** — lunar day  
2. **Nekatha** — Moon mansion  
3. **Yoga** — Sun–Moon combination  
4. **Karana** — half-tithi  
5. **Vara** — weekday  

Avoid **Rahu Kalaya** (and often Yamaganda / Gulika windows) for sensitive acts. Handbooks like *Nekath Hadana Heti* (R.H. William / Ravindra Hettige, Gunasena) teach stepwise selection.

## Implication for research → later agent

A true “experienced Handahana agent” is not only a birth-chart printer. Long-term scope includes:

- Kendraya + Nam Thebima (newborn focus — Stage 2 candidate)  
- Porondam engine (marriage)  
- Daily / event muhurta finder  
- Dasha counselling voice  

Stage 1 freezes the **knowledge**; Stage 2 chooses which slice to automate first.
